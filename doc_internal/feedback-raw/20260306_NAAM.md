# Feedback: Arrival Resources Web App

**Organization:** NAAM  
**Role** Director 
**Date:** 2026-03-06  

## Overall Response

The respondent was appreciative of the project and saw clear value in developing a guide like this for newly arrived immigrants, especially during the first years of settlement when systems, services, and everyday life can feel unfamiliar and overwhelming.

They noted that their organization had developed a similar resource guide around 20 years ago. Although it is now outdated, they remembered it being very helpful at the time for both newcomers and those supporting them. This reinforces the idea that there has long been a practical need for this kind of resource hub.

## Who It Could Be Useful For

The respondent suggested that a tool like this could be valuable for a wide range of users involved in refugee and immigrant resettlement, including:

- clients / newcomers
- service providers
- volunteers
- community partners

## Suggested Improvements / Design Priorities

1. **Focus on local resources**  
   The more geographically specific the information is, the more practical and usable it becomes for newcomers.

2. **Include community-based and low-income-accessible services**  
   The guide should include not only formal services, but also community-based resources and services that are realistically accessible to low-income populations.

3. **Support orientation use, not just directory use**  
   The guide should function not only as a resource directory but also as an orientation tool. The respondent noted that it could be used in cultural orientation sessions for newly arrived clients and shared with partner organizations such as the Department of Transitional Assistance, health clinics, food pantries, and other local providers.

4. **Make the guide as visual as possible**  
   Visual presentation is especially important because some newcomers may have limited English literacy, and some may also have limited literacy in their first language. Icons, maps, simple graphics, and clear navigation would help make the guide easier to understand and use.

## Key Takeaways

- There is strong perceived value in the project.
- A similar guide had been useful in the past, suggesting an established need for this kind of tool.
- Local specificity is critical.
- The tool may be especially useful when integrated into orientation and support workflows.
- Visual communication is essential for accessibility.
